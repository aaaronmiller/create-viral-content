## What changed, in one list

1. Platforms now demote **polished text with no perspective**, not AI use as
   such. Every draft needs a stance, a specific number or event, or a lived
   detail the author alone could supply.
2. **Engagement-keyword CTAs died.** "Comment GUIDE and I'll DM you" lost
   about half its LinkedIn reach from 2026-07-27. A plain question is fine.
3. **Links cost reach** on LinkedIn (about 16-21%) and, for non-Premium
   accounts, on X. Moving the link to the first comment no longer avoids it.
4. **Originality is enforced on video.** Narrating someone else's footage
   does not count as original on YouTube Shorts (2026-10-01).
5. **Shocked faces and short titles are not what breaks out.** See
   `research-statistics.md`, section "2026 Updates".
6. **The em dash is no longer a reliable AI tell.** Current models barely
   use it. See `ai-tells.md`, section "Current-Model Tells (2026)".
7. **EU AI Act Article 50 applies from 2026-08-02** to AI-generated text on
   public-interest topics. See [Disclosure rules in force](#disclosure-rules-in-force).

## LinkedIn

- **[VERIFIED] "Seems like AI slop" report button and slop classifiers**
  (announced 2026-07-30). Classifiers limit out-of-network reach for
  "low-effort content that may be polished but lacks an identifiable
  perspective or substantive value." LinkedIn says AI-assisted writing is
  allowed; the target is perspective-free polish. Members should disclose
  substantial AI involvement when it is not apparent.
- **[VERIFIED] First results** (2026-08-20): 1M+ members used the button in
  two weeks; classified slop gets about 40% fewer views; authors with many
  reports are notified.
- **[STUDY] MagicPost, 687k posts** (2026-10-06): "Comment KEYWORD" closers
  went from +10% to -47% against each creator's baseline; other comment
  requests -19%; closing questions unchanged; posts without concrete detail
  -23%. Link card -19%, URL in text -21%, "link in comments" -16%. Polls -22%
  against photos; carousel lead over photos down to +9%. Posting hour moved
  final reach by only ±2%; posts keep accruing views for days (72% by day 3).
- Rules: open with the author's claim or experience; end on a statement or a
  plain question; no keyword-DM bait; put the link last or leave it out;
  stop optimizing post time.

## X

- **[VERIFIED] Mutual-follow boost** (2026-07-13): posts and replies between
  accounts that follow each other rank higher. Replies inside a niche
  cluster beat cold replies under large accounts.
- **[VERIFIED] The product head who denied link deboosting left**
  (2026-08-05). **[STUDY] Buffer:** median engagement on link posts from
  non-Premium accounts has been 0% since March 2025.
- Rules: substance in the post, link second (reply or quote-post); build
  replies within the author's mutual network.

## YouTube

- **[VERIFIED] Inauthentic-content monetization policy reworded**
  (2026-07-13) into three demonetized categories: templated or
  mass-produced content (including generic AI templates without the
  creator's own insight), unsatisfying content, and AI personas on
  sensitive topics such as health or finance.
- **[VERIFIED] Shorts cuts reach for re-uploaders** (2026-10-01). Voiceover
  that only describes the footage, minor edits and template edits do not
  count as transformation.
- **[VERIFIED] Title and thumbnail A/B tests pick winners by watch time,
  not CTR.** Variants must make different promises the video keeps.
- **[VERIFIED] AI labels** sit under the player and on Shorts; YouTube runs
  its own detection, and disclosure alone does not limit reach.
- Rules: scripts carry the creator's own analysis; avoid series packaging
  that looks templated across a channel; disclose realistic synthetic media.

## Instagram and Threads

- **[VERIFIED]** Originality rules extend to photos and carousels;
  aggregators lose recommendations (2026-04-30). Commentary, remixes,
  voiceover and collabs count as original; watermarks and crops do not.
- **[VERIFIED]** Instagram is testing 90-minute "Features" videos fed by
  Reels (2026-10-26). For testers, a Reel can act as a trailer.
- **[VERIFIED]** Threads removed link-click stats (2026-09-08); add UTM
  parameters. Threads posts now surface in Google Discover, so write the
  first line as a headline.
- **[VERIFIED]** Meta auto-labels ads made with its AI tools or carrying
  C2PA metadata (around 2026-07-09).
- Rule: optimize Reels copy for sends to a friend, not likes.

## Reddit

- **[VERIFIED]** Subreddit AI bans are spreading and getting broader:
  r/opensource banned AI-made and AI-related posts and added a one-year
  account-age minimum (2026-10-26). Moderators say they cannot tell AI text
  from AI-translated text.
- **[STUDY] Pangram** (1M posts, July 2026): Reddit replies were 98% human,
  so AI-sounding comments stand out most there.
- **[VERIFIED]** Bot labels and targeted human verification for bot-like
  posting (2026-03-25).
- Rules: read the subreddit's AI rule before drafting; never post templated
  content at volume; write replies in the author's own register.

## TikTok and Bluesky

- **[LOW]** No verified ranking change after June 2026 on either.
- **[VERIFIED]** TikTok joined the C2PA steering committee (2026-07-27);
  realistic AI content still needs the AIGC label, which TikTok says does
  not cut organic reach. Viewers can filter AI content from their feeds.
- Bluesky ranks replies by social proximity: community replies beat
  broadcast posts.

## Email

- **[VERIFIED rollout, LOW effect]** Gmail's AI Inbox reached paid
  Workspace accounts from 2026-10-12. Gemini summarizes the opening lines,
  so the preheader and first sentence carry the pitch. Judge subject-line
  tests on clicks and replies, not opens.

## Detection: what not to promise

- **[VERIFIED]** Pangram raised $9M (2026-07-29) and claims its model
  catches AI-assisted, mixed and "humanized" text (vendor claim).
- **[STUDY]** Pangram measured fully AI-written shares of 41% for long
  LinkedIn posts and 29% for long X posts (April to June 2026).
- Rule: never promise that a rewrite pass evades detectors. The goal is
  substance and the author's real voice; that is also what the platform
  classifiers reward.

## Disclosure rules in force

| Rule | In force | What it requires |
|---|---|---|
| EU AI Act Article 50 | 2026-08-02 | AI-generated text published on matters of public interest needs a clear label, unless a person performed substantive editorial review and takes editorial responsibility. Text is judged by publication date, so older AI drafts published now count. Deepfake image, audio and video must be labelled. |
| California AI Transparency Act (SB 942 / AB 853) | operative 2026-08-02 | Duties fall on AI providers now; platform provenance display from 2027-01-01. No direct creator duty yet. |
| New York synthetic-performer ad law | 2026-06-09 | Ads using an AI-generated performer need a conspicuous disclosure. |
| India IT Rules amendment | 2026-02-20 | Realistic AI audio and visual content must be labelled. |
| South Korea AI Basic Act | 2026-01-22 | Watermarks on generative output; visible labels on realistic deepfakes. |
| US FTC | no new 2026 rule | Fake reviews and testimonials, AI-written or not, are banned; paid endorsements need disclosure. |
| Platforms | ongoing | YouTube and TikTok: label realistic synthetic media. LinkedIn: disclose substantial AI involvement when not apparent. Reddit: per subreddit. |

Rules for this skill:

1. For EU-facing public-interest posts (politics, health, economics), either
   a named human edits and takes responsibility, or the post carries an AI
   label. Say which applies.
2. Never write fake reviews, testimonials or invented personal experiences.
3. Paid or sponsored content always gets a sponsorship disclosure.
